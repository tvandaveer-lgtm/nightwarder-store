What this file is for

Volunteer and small combination houses that run a Tuesday-night drill or a mutual-aid tabletop need a sterile place for objectives, attendance, and what to fix next — without treating a store kit as HSEEP certification or a graded federal filing.

What’s inside

Blank drill header, type checks, and notes rows. Gold cells stay empty until your instructor fills them. Complements the incident AAR and the training cert tracker — it does not replace either.

What it is not

It is not HSEEP certification. It is not a completed graded exercise filing. It is not a federal after-action and not a capability claim. NightWarder does not invent exercise grades for a named house.
