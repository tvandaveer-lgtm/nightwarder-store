In short

NightWarder’s rope kit is a blank Excel log for life-safety rope inventory, use, and inspections — not a completed log and not a certification that you meet any NFPA edition.

Blank template for [Department] to adopt and fill. Not legal advice. Not a completed report or authorization. Not OSHA law. Not AHJ approval. Not an audit.

What this workbook is for

Volunteer departments that keep life-safety rope need a place for inventory, use history, and inspection notes without pretending a spreadsheet is a certification.

What’s inside

This workbook is built as a blank log for your department. Gold cells stay empty until your officer fills them. One file covers rope ID and in-service data, use rows (date, location, how used, conditions, damage/shock/edges), and clean/inspect results.

What it is not

It is not a manufacturer paper card in the bag. It is not proof you meet any NFPA edition. Retirement and inspection intervals stay yours and your AHJ’s — NightWarder does not print those as law.

EXAMPLE (Station 99 only): one row per rope; officer fills gold cells after each use or inspect.
