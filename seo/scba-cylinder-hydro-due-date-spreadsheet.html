In short

A blank spreadsheet to list SCBA cylinders and the due dates your department enters — not a hydrostatic test record and not automatic compliance.

Blank template for [Department] to adopt and fill. Not legal advice. Not a completed report or authorization. Not OSHA law. Not AHJ approval. Not an audit.

Track bottles by serial

Track bottles by serial without baking retirement math into the file. You enter hydro and other due dates from manufacturer data and your AHJ rules.

You enter due dates

This is a blank register for your department. The sheet stores what you type. It does not decide the date, and it does not mean a bottle is legal to fill.

EXAMPLE (Station 99 only): officer pastes a serial and a due date from house records; gold cells stay empty until then.

Not a hydro lab report

It is not a hydrostatic test lab report. NightWarder does not hardcode hydro years as OSHA or NFPA fact.

Disclaimer

A filled due-date cell is not automatic compliance. The sheet does not keep you legal.
