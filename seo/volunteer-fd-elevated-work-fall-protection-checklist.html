What this checklist is for

Volunteer and small combination houses that put members on a roof, ladder, or platform need a sterile pre-work list — harness, anchor, spotter, weather — without treating a store kit as OSHA certification or a finished permit.

What’s inside

Blank checklist rows. Gold cells stay empty until your officer fills them from the real job. Complements the permit-to-work cover — it does not replace it.

What it is not

It is not OSHA certification. It is not a completed permit. It is not a written fall-protection program. NightWarder does not invent job conditions or isolation steps for a named house.
