Blank template for [Department] to adopt and fill. Not legal advice. Not a completed report or authorization. Not OSHA law. Not AHJ approval. Not an audit.

What this workbook is for

Volunteer and small combination houses that keep a station cash box need a place for in / out / running-balance rows — without treating a spreadsheet as the monthly treasurer report or as accounting advice.

What’s inside

Blank Excel-style cash / petty rows. Gold cells stay empty until your treasurer types them. Complements the monthly treasurer report and the expense reimbursement request — it does not replace either.

What it is not

It is not a completed book. It is not a treasurer report. It is not accounting advice. NightWarder does not invent cash-box balances for a named house.

EXAMPLE (Station 99 only): sample rows use fake 2099 figures. They are not a real cash book.
