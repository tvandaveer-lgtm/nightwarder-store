What this checklist is for

Volunteer and small combination houses that drill or work at a pond, river, or ice edge need a sterile pre-work list — PFD, throw bag, shore safety — without treating a store kit as a boat license or a finished rescue narrative.

What’s inside

Blank checklist rows. Gold cells stay empty until your officer fills them from the real job or drill. Complements the elevated-work checklist and the incident AAR — it does not replace either.

What it is not

It is not USCG certification. It is not a completed rescue report. It is not a dive cert and not a boat license. NightWarder does not invent a water rescue for a named house.
