Who uses it

PPE officers already track who owns which set. Advanced cleaning and repair history is a different job — and free checklists often stop at pass/fail.

Works with inventory by serial

This blank Excel log is for recording inspections, cleanings, and repairs on turnout gear for your department. Link rows to your inventory by serial.

It does not replace the PPE / SCBA inventory tracker. For inventory today, use the live tracker on the catalog card.

Routine vs advanced are buyer labels

Labels like routine vs advanced are fields you choose; NightWarder does not print a required cadence.

Disclaimer

It is not a completed PPE report and not proof of compliance. Filling the sheet does not prove you met any standard.
