What this file is for

Volunteer and small combination houses need a sterile application the membership officer can print — name, availability, interest, and signature blanks — without treating a store kit as an accepted member file.

What’s inside

Blank application fields. Gold cells stay empty until your officer fills them. Complements the member meeting packet — it does not replace bylaws or a background check.

What it is not

It is not a completed membership record. It is not legal advice. NightWarder does not pre-fill applicants or invent a roster for a named house.
