What this checklist is for

Volunteer and small combination houses that want a sterile pre-use list for a facepiece — inspect, cartridge or cylinder note, user seal check — without treating a store kit as a written program, a medical file, or a fit-test card.

What’s inside

Blank checklist rows. Gold cells stay empty until your officer fills them from the real set. Complements the PPE / SCBA tracker — it does not replace inventory, and it does not become a respiratory program.

What it is not

Checklist only. It is not a full respiratory program. It is not a medical evaluation. It is not a fit-test certificate. It is not OSHA 1910.134 compliance. NightWarder does not print medical clearance, fit-test intervals, or program language as law.
