In short

A blank spreadsheet to list SCBA cylinders and the due dates your department enters — not a hydrostatic test record and not automatic compliance.

Track bottles by serial — no hardcoded retirement math

Each cylinder gets a row and a serial. You can see what is in the rack. The workbook does not bake in a retirement year or a hydro interval. Those numbers change by cylinder type, manufacturer, and who you answer to. We do not invent them.

You enter due dates from your AHJ and manufacturer rules

Look at the stamp, the lab report, or the rule your AHJ uses. Type that due date. The sheet stores what you typed. It does not decide the date for you, and it does not mean the bottle is legal to fill.

EXAMPLE only: Station 99 would paste a serial and a due date from its own records — the gold cells stay empty until then.

Not a hydro lab report

A hydrostatic test record comes from the lab that tested the cylinder. This file is a register you keep in the house. It is not that lab report, and it is not a substitute for one.
