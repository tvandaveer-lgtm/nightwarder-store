In short

A blank Word entry permit with attendant log for your department to adopt — not a completed permit, not AHJ approval, and not a full confined-space program.

Permit fields and attendant roster in one packet

The live kit is a blank entry permit plus an attendant log. One packet so the officer is not hunting two forms. Gold fields stay empty until your people write in them.

File by email after Stripe checkout. Support: nightwarder@agentmail.to.

Built as a blank form — you fill acceptable conditions

Atmosphere rows, isolation notes, and rescue notes are INPUT blanks. Do not invent O2, LEL, or toxic numbers. Write only what a real test showed. Acceptable ranges are local / AHJ — blank until you set them.

EXAMPLE only: Station 99 sample shots show empty gold cells and toy labels. They are not a filled permit.

Not a written PRCS program

This is not a written permit-required confined space program. Not OSHA law. Not a completed authorization. Not AHJ approval. If your department cannot support entry rescue as required locally, do not treat this form as permission to go in.

Energy isolation / lockout is a separate process. A status note on the permit is not a full LOTO procedure.

When you also need hot work or a PTW cover

Hot work on or near the space is a different authorization. Use the hot work permit if that is the job. A permit-to-work cover does not replace this entry permit or a hot work permit. Browse safety permits for the live cards.
