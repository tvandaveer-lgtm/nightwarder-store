In short

NightWarder PPE/SCBA tracker is a blank Excel workbook for gear inventory and inspection dates — not OSHA certification and not a finished PPE report.

Inventory, inspections, fit-test log, due dashboard

The live kit is a workbook for the gear on the floor: SCBA, masks, turnout, and the rest you list. You can log inspections and fit tests, and keep a due view from dates you entered. Gold cells are INPUT.

File by email after Stripe checkout. Support: nightwarder@agentmail.to.

Fit-test months are your INPUT

Any “months until next” field is a number you type. Confirm the interval with your AHJ. The sample screenshot on the catalog uses fake Station 99 figures — including a placeholder month count. That number is not a recommendation.

EXAMPLE only: Station 99 sample rows use toy serials and 2099 dates. Do not copy them into a working sheet.

Not a respiratory written program

This is not a written respiratory protection program. It is not OSHA certification. It is not a completed PPE report. You still need whatever program, trainer, and AHJ sign-off your department already uses.

Pair later with the turnout advanced log

Need a longer cleaning and repair history by serial? That is a separate blank workbook — see the turnout advanced inspection and cleaning log. It is not live on the catalog yet. The tracker stays the inventory. A cylinder due-date register is also planned as its own blank file.
