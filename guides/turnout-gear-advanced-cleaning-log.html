In short

This is a blank Excel log for recording turnout inspections, cleanings, and repairs — not OSHA/NFPA proof and not a completed PPE report.

Who uses it

The person who tracks bunker gear — often the PPE officer. They need a sheet that follows a coat or pair of pants after a wash, a repair, or a closer look. This file is that sheet, empty.

Works with inventory by serial

Link a row to the same serial you already keep on the PPE / SCBA tracker. This log does not replace that kit. The tracker is the inventory. This workbook is the longer cleaning and repair history, once it is live.

If you only need who has which coat right now, start with the live tracker on the catalog.

Routine vs advanced are buyer labels

The workbook may have columns you label “routine” or “advanced.” Those words are yours. They are not a printed legal cadence. We do not say an NFPA edition requires a certain month or a certain cleaner. If your ISP, manufacturer, or AHJ uses those words, you write what they asked for.
