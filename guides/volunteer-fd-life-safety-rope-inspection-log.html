In short

NightWarder’s rope kit is a blank Excel log for life-safety rope inventory, use, and inspections — not a completed log and not a certification that you meet any NFPA edition.

What this workbook is for

If the house keeps life-safety rope, someone has to know which lines are in service, when they went out, and when they were inspected. This workbook is that blank place. Inventory, use, and inspect — one file, not three clipboards.

It does not tell you how often to inspect. That call stays with your department and your AHJ.

What’s inside

Sheets for inventory, use, and inspections. Gold cells are INPUT — empty until you type. No dates or intervals ship as rules.

  • A row per rope, once you add it
  • A use log you fill after work or training
  • An inspection log you fill when you look at the line

EXAMPLE only: Station 99 keeps one row per rope; gold cells stay empty until the officer fills them.

What it is not

It is not a completed log. It is not a certification that you meet any NFPA edition. We do not print a retirement year, a monthly cadence, or a manufacturer interval as law. If your AHJ or the rope maker gives you a rule, you write that rule in.

How it differs from a manufacturer paper card

A paper card in the bag is one rope, one clip. Fine for that bag. This is a workbook: many ropes, one file the officer can keep with the rest of the house paperwork. It does not replace the card if your manufacturer still wants one in the bag.
